# Organic Chemistry – IIT-JEE Notes

## 1. General Organic Chemistry (GOC)

### Key Concepts
- **Hybridization**: sp³ (tetrahedral, 109.5°), sp² (trigonal planar, 120°), sp (linear, 180°)
- **Inductive Effect**:
  - −I: −NO₂, −CN, −COOH, −F, −Cl, −Br, −I, −OH, −OR
  - +I: Alkyl groups (−CH₃ < −C₂H₅ < −C₃H₇)
- **Resonance / Mesomeric Effect**:
  - +M (electron donating): −OH, −OR, −NH₂, −NHR
  - −M (electron withdrawing): −NO₂, −CN, −CHO, −COOH
- **Hyperconjugation**: σ-bond electrons delocalize into adjacent empty p orbital.
  - Stability of carbocations: 3° > 2° > 1° > CH₃⁺
- **Electrophilic Aromatic Substitution (EAS)**:
  - Activating groups (o/p directors): −OH, −NH₂, −OR, −NHCOR, alkyl
  - Deactivating groups (m directors): −NO₂, −CN, −COOH, −CHO, −SO₃H
  - Halogens: deactivating but o/p directors

### Common JEE Traps
- Steric effects can override electronic effects in certain reactions.
- Resonance is more important than inductive effect when both operate.
- Carbanion stability: opposite to carbocation (EWG stabilizes).

## 2. Hydrocarbons

### Alkanes
- **Preparation**: Wurtz reaction, Kolbe's electrolysis, decarboxylation.
- **Reactions**: Free-radical halogenation (reactivity: 3°H > 2°H > 1°H).
- **Combustion**: CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ + (3n+1)/2 O₂ → nCO₂ + (n+1)H₂O

### Alkenes
- **Preparation**: Dehydration of alcohols (Saytzeff's rule), dehydrohalogenation.
- **Reactions**:
  - Electrophilic addition: Markovnikov's rule (HX), Anti-Markovnikov (HBr + peroxide)
  - Ozonolysis: alkene → aldehydes/ketones
  - Dihydroxylation: KMnO₄ (syn) or OsO₄ (syn)

### Alkynes
- **Preparation**: Dehydrohalogenation (2 equiv), CaC₂ + H₂O → C₂H₂
- **Reactions**: Similar to alkenes but two additions possible.
  - Acidic hydrogen: terminal alkynes react with NaNH₂, AgNO₃, Cu₂Cl₂
  - Lindlar's catalyst: partial hydrogenation → cis-alkene
  - Na/liq. NH₃: → trans-alkene

## 3. Alkyl Halides

### Key Concepts
- **SN1 vs SN2**:
  - SN2: strong nucleophile, 1° substrate, polar aprotic solvent, inversion
  - SN1: weak nucleophile, 3° substrate, polar protic solvent, racemization
- **E1 vs E2**:
  - E2: strong base, anti-periplanar geometry, Saytzeff
  - E1: weak base, 3° substrate, carbocation intermediate

### Important Reactions
- **Grignard reagent**: RMgX + various electrophiles
- **Wurtz reaction**: 2RX + 2Na → R−R + 2NaX
- **Finkelstein reaction**: RCl + NaI → RI + NaCl (acetone solvent)
- **Swarts reaction**: RCl + AgF → RF + AgCl

## 4. Alcohols, Phenols & Ethers

### Alcohols
- **Acidity order**: Phenol > Water > 1° alcohol > 2° > 3°
- **Lucas test**: Differentiates 1°, 2°, 3° alcohols (ZnCl₂/HCl)
  - 3°: immediate turbidity, 2°: 5 min, 1°: requires heating
- **Reactions**:
  - Dehydration: H₂SO₄, follows Saytzeff
  - Oxidation: PCC (→aldehyde), KMnO₄ (→carboxylic acid)
  - Esterification: ROH + RCOOH ⇌ RCOOR + H₂O

### Phenols
- **More acidic than alcohols** due to resonance stabilization of phenoxide ion.
- **Kolbe's reaction**: Phenol + NaOH + CO₂ → Salicylic acid
- **Reimer-Tiemann reaction**: Phenol + CHCl₃/NaOH → Salicylaldehyde
- **Fries rearrangement**: Phenyl ester → o/p-hydroxy ketone (Lewis acid)

## 5. Aldehydes, Ketones & Carboxylic Acids

### Aldehydes & Ketones
- **Nucleophilic Addition**: HCN, NaHSO₃, Grignard, amines (→ imine/Schiff base)
- **Aldol condensation**: 2 × aldehyde with α-H → β-hydroxy aldehyde → α,β-unsaturated aldehyde
- **Cannizzaro reaction**: Aldehyde without α-H + conc. NaOH → acid + alcohol
- **Wittig reaction**: Aldehyde/ketone + phosphorus ylide → alkene
- **Tollen's test**: Ag⁺ → Ag (silver mirror) — detects aldehydes
- **Fehling's test**: Cu²⁺ → Cu₂O (red ppt) — detects aldehydes (not aromatic)

### Carboxylic Acids
- **Acidity**: Effect of substituents (EWG ↑ acidity, EDG ↓ acidity)
- **Hell-Volhard-Zelinsky (HVZ)**: α-halogenation of carboxylic acids
- **Decarboxylation**: RCOONa + NaOH + CaO → RH + Na₂CO₃

## 6. Amines

### Key Concepts
- **Basicity**: Aliphatic amines > NH₃ > Aromatic amines
  - In gas phase: 3° > 2° > 1° > NH₃
  - In aqueous: 2° > 1° > 3° > NH₃ (due to solvation)
- **Preparation**: Reduction of nitro compounds, Gabriel synthesis, Hofmann bromamide.
- **Carbylamine test**: 1° amine + CHCl₃ + KOH → isocyanide (foul smell)
- **Diazotization**: ArNH₂ + NaNO₂ + HCl → ArN₂⁺Cl⁻ (0–5°C)
  - Sandmeyer reaction: ArN₂⁺ + CuX → ArX
  - Coupling: ArN₂⁺ + phenol/amine → azo dye
